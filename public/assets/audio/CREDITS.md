# Field recordings

The following recordings were released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- The river bed uses a filtered, crossfaded excerpt of Thimras’s small-river recording from [Park ambiences](https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences).
- The canopy calls are excerpts from isaiah658’s [Ambient Bird Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-bird-sounds).
- The four paddle strokes are filtered cuts from Joseph Sardin’s [Rowing Quickly](https://bigsoundbank.com/rowing-quickly-s1513.html).
